# BPEI Flagship Explainer Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Cinematic enterprise systems interface combining BPEI fire/ice polarity, black architectural space, metallic accents, and P.O.L.A.R. operational-interface language. The composition should feel engineered rather than advertised: controlled, technical, high-contrast, dimensional, and unconventional without becoming chaotic.

## Colors
- #05080E — primary canvas
- #00E5FF — ice / systems signal
- #FF5500 — fire / disruption signal
- #FFB300 — molten gold / institutional accent
- #F6F6F2 — primary typography

## Typography
- DejaVu Sans Bold or approved BPEI geometric sans equivalent for display
- DejaVu Sans or approved BPEI sans equivalent for supporting copy

## Motion
- Controlled cinematic drift
- 0.7s scene transitions
- No jump cuts
- Entrances precede hero frames
- Preserve narrator audio benchmark timing

## What NOT to Do
- No generic blue SaaS gradients
- No rejected or unapproved brand imagery
- No random stock footage
- No cartoonish bounce motion
- No substitute narrator voice
